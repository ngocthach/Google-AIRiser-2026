   - WILL FAIL IF:
   - OUT OF SCOPE:

01 · Ideation
The artifact — 5-line decision record

The PistonCraft PRD & 7 Core Parts

5-Line Decision Record

  • Concept: PistonCraft (Diagnostic Lab + Assembly Bay + Dyno Test)
  • Target: Ages 8–16 (Visual, tactile, mobile touch-friendly)
  • Why This Wins: High tactile gratification — every part mounts visibly onto the engine block with real HP increases
  • Will Fail If: Part snapping feels clunky or engine audio feels fake
  • Out of Scope: Complex 3D CAD models, multiplayer servers, gearboxes

The 7 Core Collectible Parts

  • 1. Engine Block (Cast iron foundation & cylinders)
  • 2. Crankshaft (Converts reciprocating to rotational motion)
  • 3. Pistons & Rods (Contains combustion force & compression)
  • 4. Cylinder Head & Valves (Controls air intake & exhaust gas)
  • 5. Spark Plugs (High-voltage ignition arc)
  • 6. Intake Manifold (Air-fuel mixture distribution)
  • 7. Turbocharger (Forced induction via exhaust energy)
