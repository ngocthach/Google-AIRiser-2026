02 · Before you build
Read this first

Your users include 6-year-olds

That single fact changes the design. Getting it wrong builds something genuinely harmful. Two rules are non-negotiable.

Rule 1

Age tiers, not one app for everyone

A 6-year-old and a 20-year-old cannot have the same account capabilities.

Rule 2

Do not build payments

Campaigns collect pledges — promises, not transactions.

02 · Rule 1
The single most important line

Never allow private 1-to-1 messaging
between different age bands

Instead

  • All team discussion in one shared thread
  • Every team member can see it
  • For teams with under-16s, a teacher is in the thread

Never public

  • Precise location
  • Phone numbers or email
  • Face photo combined with a school name

This one design choice removes the largest risk in any platform that connects minors with strangers. Your AI moderation step enforces the rest automatically.

02 · Rule 2
Rule 2 — the most important engineering advice here

Pledges, not payments

A pledge is a promise — money, materials, mentoring, or hours. Fulfilment happens off-platform through the school. This is not a cop-out:

Legal

Minors cannot enter binding contracts. Handling other people's money means KYC, licensing, and refund liability.

Practical

Payment integration is the fastest way for a beginner MVP to stall — the whole budget gone, nothing to show.

It demos identically

Judges see the same loop: campaign → goal → pledges → progress. Nothing is missing.

If real money is ever needed later: route it through a licensed provider with the school or a guardian as merchant of record — never the child.

05 · Step 2
Step 2 — Build · the master prompt (1 of 3)

Role, task & the safety rule

System Role: You are an expert full-stack developer and UX/UI designer
who builds safe, accessible products for children and teenagers.

Task: Build a complete, working web application called "Ươm Mầm" — a
platform where students aged 6 to 22 share project ideas, form teams,
and gather community support.

Tech Stack: React single-page app, Tailwind CSS, mobile-first.

THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE — AGE TIERS.
On first open the user picks an age band. This controls what they can do:
- 6-12 ("Lớp học"): joins with a class code. No public profile, no photos,
  no messaging, no campaigns. Sees only ideas sharing their class code.
- 13-15: posts publicly after moderation. Joins teams. No private
  messages. No campaigns.
- 16-17: public ideas, teams, campaigns badged "Cần người giám hộ xác nhận".
- 18+: all features.
Never allow private one-to-one messaging between different age bands.
All team discussion happens in one shared thread visible to every member.
05 · Step 2
Step 2 — Build · the master prompt (2 of 3)

Screens, pledges & data

Core screens (exactly four):
1. Bảng ý tưởng — grid of idea cards, filter by category and age band
2. Chi tiết ý tưởng — full idea, team members, "Xin tham gia" button
3. Đội của tôi — teams I own or joined, with a shared update thread
4. Chiến dịch ủng hộ — campaign page with a goal and a list of pledges

IMPORTANT: campaigns collect PLEDGES, not payments.
A pledge is a promise of money, materials, mentoring, or hours.
There is no checkout, no card form, and no payment processing
anywhere in this app. Show pledge progress toward the goal.

Data: use browser localStorage. Seed the app with 8 sample ideas
across different age bands and categories so it is never empty
on first open.
05 · Step 2
Step 2 — Build · the master prompt (3 of 3)

AI features, accessibility & vibe

AI features (use Gemini):
- "Trợ lý ý tưởng": the user types a rough idea in any wording. Gemini returns
  a structured card (Vấn đề / Ai gặp / Giải pháp / Bước đầu tiên) and asks 3 short
  questions to sharpen it.
- "Đọc theo tuổi": a toggle that rewrites any idea at the reader's reading level —
  simple words for a 7-year-old, normal language for a teenager.
- "Đội cần ai": from an idea, suggest the 3 roles the team needs.
- "Kiểm duyệt": before anything is published, Gemini checks for personal contact
  details, unsafe content, or bullying, and blocks it with a friendly explanation.

Accessibility (required): every action reachable by keyboard · WCAG AA contrast ·
large tap targets, one-handed on a phone · alt text on every image · every progress
bar also shows its value as text.

The Vibe: bright, friendly, encouraging. Rounded cards, warm colours, generous
whitespace. A school project fair, not a corporate dashboard. UI language: Vietnamese.

Output: complete working code. No placeholders, no "// add logic here".
05 · Step 2
Why that prompt is so long

Safety is inside the prompt

The common way

Build the app first. Add safety rules afterwards, when the data model already assumes everyone is equal.

This way

The AI builds age tiers into the very first render. Safety is structural, not a patch.

Sophistication in equals quality out. This is the handbook's core lesson — and the reason your app will feel finished while others feel like demos.

06 · Step 3
Step 3 — Iterate

The Layered Build Approach

Core logic first, then aesthetics, then AI features. Never ask for everything at once — send one layer, test, then send the next.

Layer 1

Join flow

Request to join · owner accepts or declines · accepted members appear on the team.

Layer 2

Team updates

Shared progress thread, newest first, author + age band. No private messages.

Layer 3

The campaign

Title, story, needs, goal, deadline. Pledge type: Tiền · Vật liệu · Cố vấn · Thời gian.

Layer 4

The AI coach

After 3 questions, regenerate a polished idea card and publish in one click.

Layer 5

Visible moderation

Blocked posts explain which rule and how to fix. A shield icon = "đã kiểm duyệt".

Layer 6

Accessibility pass

Keyboard nav, focus rings, ARIA labels, AA contrast, text equivalents.

06 · Step 3
Three techniques that save hours

Stop typing. Start directing.

Fix

Let it self-heal

Hit an error? Click Fix and let Gemini diagnose and repair. Never debug by hand.

Annotate

Draw the change

Click "Annotate app", draw on the part you want changed, describe it. Far faster than words.

Architect Interview

Ask before it codes

"Ask me 5 technical questions about the user flow before you start coding."

The Architect Interview catches mismatches before they become code you have to unpick.

07 · Step 4
The moment that matters

localStorage → Firestore
is when safety becomes real

Add Firebase Authentication with "Đăng nhập bằng Google" and move idea,
team, and pledge storage from localStorage to Firestore, so different
users on different devices see the same ideas.
Keep all the age-band rules enforced on read and write.

Until now your app had one user: you. After this change it is multi-user — and your age rules stop being cosmetic. Re-test every age tier. Rules enforced only in the UI are not enforced at all.

10 · Scoring
What the judges actually reward

40 / 30 / 30

40%
FEASIBILITY

The largest slice — and the one you fully control. A small app where every button works beats an ambitious broken one. Accessibility is named here. Don't skip Layer 6.

30%
IMPACT

Name your users precisely. Not "students" but "lớp 4–5 tại trường tiểu học công lập, nơi giáo viên muốn học sinh làm dự án nhóm nhưng không có công cụ an toàn."

30%
CREATIVITY

Your differentiators: age-adaptive reading and moderation as a friendly visible feature. Lead with those.

State the age-tier design and the pledges-not-payments decision explicitly. Judges reward understanding constraints far more than pretending they don't exist.

13 · Beyond
If you keep going after the competition

In priority order

01

A real teacher flow

Class codes, roster import, teacher dashboard. Schools are the only realistic distribution channel for a 6–12 product.

02

Human moderation queue

AI catches the obvious; a person must handle the ambiguous. Any platform with minors needs a report button and a human behind it.

03

Guardian consent records

Proper stored consent for under-16 accounts — before you have real users, not after.

04

Outcome tracking

How many teams actually shipped something? That number is what makes this fundable — and no competitor will have it.
